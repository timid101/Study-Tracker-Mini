# Offline Android Study Tracker v2.0 (User-Defined Subjects, Per-Subject Timers, General Settings & Home Screen Widget)

## What's New in v2.0
1. **100% Free User Choice of Subjects & Targets**: Starts completely clean with no hardcoded courses. Add any subject/course, set its target study hours, edit target hours anytime, or delete subjects.
2. **First Interface (`Subjects` Tab)**: Displays your list of subjects with **Target Hours**, **Completed Time**, **Remaining Time**, and **Completion Percentage** (`%` bar), plus quick buttons to start that subject's timer or log manual hours.
3. **Second Interface (`Timers` Tab — Head of Subjects & Timers of Each Subject)**: Every subject has its own dedicated timer card (Stopwatch or Countdown limit) with live remaining-time tracking and lock-screen notification controls.
4. **Third Interface (`Settings` Tab — General Settings)**: Default timer mode, default countdown duration, auto-stop when 100% target is reached, Home Screen Widget sync, progress reset, and offline `.json` / `.csv` Excel export.
5. **Native Android Home Screen Widget (`StudyTrackerWidgetProvider`)**: Pin the resizable AMOLED widget to your phone's home screen to see all your course names and the exact percentage (`%`) of your target study hours completed.
